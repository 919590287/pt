## Design Context

### Users
The platform is an enterprise-facing digital twin product used for external demonstration. Visitors are primarily stakeholders, clients, and decision makers evaluating the platform's visualization capability and overall technical maturity.

### Brand Personality
Concise, premium, and data-confident. The interface should feel polished and credible, with a calm executive-demo tone rather than a dense internal operations-console tone.

### Aesthetic Direction
A clean, high-end blue visualization panel system. The visual language should support digital twin presentation: refined spatial rhythm, clear data hierarchy, restrained glow/tech effects, and purposeful map/data contrast.

### Design Principles
- Lead with visual comprehension: users should understand the data story at a glance.
- Keep the interface premium and restrained: avoid noisy dashboards, generic card grids, and decorative effects that do not clarify data.
- Use blue as a disciplined brand/data system, with enough neutral contrast and accent color to prevent a flat one-note panel.
- Prioritize external presentation quality: every panel should look intentional in a demo or client-facing setting.
- Make visualization states legible: loading, empty, hover, selected, and error states should communicate confidence rather than expose implementation detail.
